# Data Professional Survey Breakdown

**Tool:** Power BI

## Overview
A Power BI dashboard analyzing a global survey of 630 data professionals, covering salary, career sentiment, programming language preferences, and geographic distribution to benchmark the respondent base.

## Key Metrics
- Total survey takers: **630** | Average age: **29.87**
- Happiness with work/life balance: **5.74 / 10** | Happiness with salary: **4.27 / 10**
- Average salary by job title: Data Scientist (94), Data Engineer (65), Data Architect (64), Other (60)
- Salary by gender: Male ~55.1 vs. Female ~53.4 (in thousands)
- Most popular programming language: **Python**, followed by R and Other
- Difficulty breaking into data: Difficult (42.7%), Easy (24.76%), Neither easy nor difficult (21%), Very Difficult (6.98%)
- Top countries: United States, followed by India, United Kingdom, and Canada

## Key Insight
Salary satisfaction (4.27/10) trails work-life balance satisfaction (5.74/10), and 42.7% of respondents found breaking into the data field "Difficult" — pointing to entry barriers as a bigger pain point than day-to-day work conditions.

## Tools & Techniques
- DAX measures for average salary, satisfaction scores, and language/country breakdowns
- Donut and gauge visuals for satisfaction and difficulty metrics
